    How to play

    Follow the beacon. Complete the five phases of the coffee production chain — and bump into the big letters, they are real.

    1. Harvest — drive through the farm and collect 10 ripe cherries.
    2. Ferment — load them into Ferroir.
    3. Drying — spread the beans on raised beds.
    4. Roasting — roast to a light profile.
    5. Packaging — seal your own bag of beans.
